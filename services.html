Custom AI and automation, built around how your team already works.

Typical examples of what we design and deploy. Every project starts from your own workflows, never from a template.

01 / 06

Invoice and document processing

The work today
Supplier invoices and other paperwork arrive by email or on paper, then get retyped into accounting software and chased for approval.
What we build
A system that captures each document, reads the key fields, checks them against your records and files them where they belong.
What changes
Nobody retypes. Exceptions go to a person, and everything else moves on its own.
02 / 06

Lead capture and qualification

The work today
Enquiries land in inboxes, messages and web forms, and get answered whenever someone has a spare moment.
What we build
One place where every enquiry is captured, scored against your own criteria, routed to the right person and followed up on schedule.
What changes
Promising leads reach the right person quickly, and none go cold without anyone noticing.
03 / 06

Reporting and dashboards

The work today
Someone exports spreadsheets every week and assembles the management report by hand.
What we build
Live dashboards and scheduled reports built straight from your own data, in the shape your managers actually use.
What changes
The numbers are there when you ask for them, and nobody spends their Friday building the report.
04 / 06

Data sync between your systems

The work today
The same customer or order is typed into the CRM, the accounting software and a spreadsheet.
What we build
Integrations that keep those systems matching, with clear rules for which record wins when they disagree.
What changes
One source of truth, no copy and paste, and fewer conversations about which number is right.
05 / 06

Customer follow-ups and reminders

The work today
Quotes go unanswered, invoices go unpaid and appointments are forgotten because chasing is manual.
What we build
Follow-ups and reminders sent on a schedule you define, with a person stepping in only when a conversation needs one.
What changes
Nothing slips through the cracks, and your team spends time on conversations rather than chasing.
06 / 06

Internal approvals and workflows

The work today
Requests move by email and chat, and nobody is sure where an approval is stuck.
What we build
Request, approval and stock or purchase workflows that route to the right people and keep a clear record.
What changes
Every request has an owner, a status and a history.
