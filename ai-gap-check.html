    What you get

    • A plain-English list of the gaps we found in how your business runs.
    • How ready each part of the business is for AI, scored out of five.
    • The projected yearly value of closing the gaps, and the return on doing so.

    What it does not include

    The free check does not put the gaps in priority order and does not give a cost estimate for each opportunity. Those come with the full AI gap assessment, where we also walk you through the findings.

    The figures are indicative. They are built from what you tell us and typical results for similar work, so treat them as a range to test, not a promise.
