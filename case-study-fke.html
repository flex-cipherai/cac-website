CASE STUDY

Fix Kenya Engineering & Construction

How we automated FKE's finance operations and recovered over 2,600 hours annually across the team and CEO.

Engineering & Construction 6 Modules + AI Assistant Existing CRM Integration
THE CHALLENGE

A growing company held back by manual finance operations.

FKE's finance department handled KES 5M in monthly revenue, 300+ supplier payments and a KES 20M debtor book — across disconnected spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and manual data entry.

The CEO spent 2–3 hours daily approving payments over WhatsApp. Cash flow visibility was limited to end-of-week spreadsheets. Budget tracking was reactive. Debtor follow-ups were inconsistent.

The operations were not broken — they were just too manual for the volume the business had reached.

WHAT WE BUILT

Six custom modules and an AI assistant — all inside FKE's existing CRM.

Transaction Sync

Automated syncing of bank transactions into the CRM, eliminating manual data entry and ensuring real-time accuracy.

Budget Control

Project-level budget tracking with automated alerts when spend approaches or exceeds thresholds. Overruns are caught in real time, not after the fact.

Digital Payment Approvals

Replaced the WhatsApp approval chain with a structured digital workflow. The CEO now reviews and approves payments in minutes, not hours.

Real-Time CEO Dashboard

A live dashboard showing cash position, project budgets, outstanding debts and payment pipelines — updated automatically.

Automated Debtor Collections

Systematic follow-up sequences for outstanding invoices, replacing ad-hoc manual chasing with consistent, automated outreach.

Payroll Compliance

Automated payroll calculations, statutory deduction tracking and compliance reporting — reducing errors and audit risk.

AI Financial Assistant

An AI assistant embedded directly in the CRM that lets the team query financial data in plain language — "What's our outstanding debt this month?" — and get instant answers.
