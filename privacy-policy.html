1. Introduction

SDFM Group Limited ("SDFM," "we," "us" or "our") designs, builds and deploys custom AI and business systems for companies operating in Kenya and beyond. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through sdfmgroup.com and our related booking and project-management tools (the "Services"), why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights you have over it.

We are a Kenyan company and our primary legal obligations arise under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 ("Kenya DPA") and its subsidiary regulations, enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner ("ODPC"). Because our website is publicly accessible, this policy also explains the additional rights available to visitors covered by the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA").

By using our website or Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website or Services.

2. Information we collect

We collect as little personal data as we need to run our business and deliver our Services. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

Information you give us directly

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number and job title, for example when you book a discovery call or submit an inquiry.
  • Business information — company name, industry, team size and details about your operations and workflows, which you share so we can scope an AI Gap Assessment or a project.
  • Project and communications data — messages, files, requirements documents and any other information you provide while we work together, including through project-management tools we use to deliver a Service.
  • Billing information — invoicing details and payment records for clients (we do not store full card numbers; these are handled by our payment processors).

Information we collect automatically

  • Technical and usage data — IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed and referring URLs, collected through standard web server logs when you visit our website.

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website. If that changes, we will update this policy and the "Cookies and tracking" section below before any such tool goes live.

3. How we collect information

  • Directly from you — when you fill out our discovery-call intake form, email us, or communicate with us during a project.
  • Automatically — through standard server logs generated as your browser interacts with our website.
  • From service providers — such as our hosting and scheduling providers, who process data on our instructions to help us run our website and booking tools.

4. How we use your information

We use personal data only for purposes connected to running our business and delivering our Services, including to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery calls and AI Gap Assessments;
  • Design, build, deploy and support the systems we are engaged to deliver;
  • Communicate with you about your project, including updates, invoices and support;
  • Maintain the security, integrity and proper functioning of our website and Services;
  • Comply with our legal, tax and accounting obligations; and
  • Enforce our agreements and protect our legal rights.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not sell your personal data.

5. Our legal basis for processing

Under the Kenya DPA and, where applicable, the GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases to process personal data:

  • Consent — where you have voluntarily provided information, such as by submitting an inquiry or booking form.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the Services you have engaged us for, including project work, support and invoicing.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure and improve our website and business, provided this does not override your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with tax, accounting or other applicable laws.

6. How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share personal data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — vetted third parties that host our website, manage scheduling/intake, or support project delivery and communications, bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants and auditors, where necessary for their services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons — where disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of SDFM, our clients or others.
  • Business transfers — if SDFM is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy or a successor policy.

7. International data transfers

We are based in Kenya, but some of our service providers (for example, cloud hosting and scheduling tools) may process or store data outside Kenya. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a level of protection consistent with the Kenya DPA and, where applicable, GDPR requirements for cross-border transfers, such as using providers with appropriate safeguards or standard contractual protections.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to deliver our Services, maintain business records, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and our relationship with you — for example, inquiry data from someone who does not become a client is retained for a limited period before deletion, while client project and billing records are retained for the period required by Kenyan tax and company law. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

9. How we protect your information

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure, including access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limiting data access to personnel who need it to do their jobs. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data appropriately protected and to respond promptly to any incident.

10. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It may use essential, strictly necessary technical storage (for example, to remember your cookie preference or menu state) required for the site to function. If we introduce analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will update this section, and where required by law, request your consent before they are set.

11. Your privacy rights

Under the Kenya DPA, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your personal data is being used;
  • Access personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal data;
  • Request deletion of personal data we no longer have a lawful basis to hold;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your personal data;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal; and
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya, if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the "Contact us" section below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

12. Additional rights for EU/UK/EEA residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have the rights described above plus the right to data portability (to receive certain data you provided us in a structured, machine-readable format) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority in addition to, or instead of, the ODPC.

13. Additional rights for California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose and (where applicable) sell; the right to request deletion of your personal information; the right to correct inaccurate personal information; and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is no "opt-out of sale/sharing" action required. You may exercise these rights using the contact details below.

14. Children's privacy

Our website and Services are directed at businesses and are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

15. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party sites, such as LinkedIn or our booking platform. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal data.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, Services or applicable law. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted, where appropriate, through the website or direct communication with active clients.
