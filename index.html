The problem

AI is everywhere, and most Kenyan businesses still can't make it work for them.

The technology exists and the willingness is there. What is missing is a partner who can see the gaps, design the right solution and deliver it. That is what SDFM does.

  1. 01

    The awareness gap

    You know AI could improve your operations, but you cannot tell which workflows would benefit most or where to start. So nothing happens.

  2. 02

    The expertise gap

    Even with a use case in mind, you lack the in-house talent to design, build and deploy it. Hiring a full-time developer is expensive and risky.

  3. 03

    The implementation gap

    Off-the-shelf tools promise transformation but deliver frustration. They do not know your workflows, your team structure or your data.

How it works

Three phases. The last one does not end.

See the full process
PHASE 01

AssessFree

A structured AI Gap Assessment of your operations, at no cost. We map your workflows, identify where AI and systemisation can save you time or money, and deliver a report with specific recommendations, projected ROI and a clear implementation plan.

PHASE 02

Build

Once you approve the plan, we get to work. Every solution is broken into milestones with progress updates at each stage. Whether it is a standalone system or a set of modules within your existing platform, we build it to spec and on schedule.

PHASE 03

Deploy & Support

We put the solution live, train your team and confirm everything is fully operational. After handover, ongoing maintenance and support are available so your systems stay sharp as your business evolves.
