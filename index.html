The problem

AI is everywhere. Most Kenyan businesses still can't make it work for them.

The technology exists and the willingness is there. Three gaps stand in the way.

  1. Awareness gap

    You can't tell where AI would help

    You know AI and better systems could improve how your business runs. What you can't tell is which workflows to start with, or what it would take. So nothing happens.

  2. Expertise gap

    You know where, but have no one to build it

    You have a clear idea of what should be automated. You have no one in-house who can build it, and hiring a full-time developer is expensive.

  3. Implementation gap

    Off-the-shelf tools don't fit how you work

    Ready-made software is built for an average business. Yours has its own steps, approvals and data, and the tool never quite matches them.
