Gaps we found (8)

Sales and lead handling

Manual lead logging and slow response times

Inbound leads from multiple channels are logged manually when staff remember, leading to a two-day response time.

You told us: We get about 40 inbound leads a month... leads are logged manually when someone remembers. We take about two working days to respond

Impact: Prospective clients experience slow replies, risking lost engagement and opportunities.

Opportunity: Automatically capture and assign inbound leads in the CRM instantly.

USD 2,780 to USD 6,070 a year | 4 h/week back

Proposals written completely from scratch

The team manually writes approximately 10 proposals a month from scratch.

You told us: write around 10 proposals a month from scratch

Impact: Consumes valuable partner and business development time on repetitive documentation.

Opportunity: Implement automated proposal generation templates linked to CRM data.

USD 3,710 to USD 8,100 a year | 5.5 h/week back

Customer communication and support

Manual client onboarding and asset collection

Account managers manually send questionnaires and chase brand assets, access, and approvals via email.

You told us: the account manager sends a Word questionnaire and chases brand assets, ad-account access and approvals by email. It takes about two weeks

Impact: Delays the time to first deliverable to two weeks and creates administrative friction for new clients.

Opportunity: Streamline onboarding with automated document collection portals and checklist workflows.

USD 4,770 to USD 10,420 a year | 7 h/week back

Core operations and workflows

Heavy reliance on large status meetings for project tracking

Project statuses go stale in Asana, requiring a 90-minute Monday status meeting with 14 people to align.

You told us: statuses go stale, so we rely on Slack and a 90-minute Monday status meeting with 14 people. Scope changes agreed on calls often go undocumented

Impact: Ties up significant staff hours in meetings and leaves scope changes undocumented.

Opportunity: Automate task status updates and sync client communications to maintain accurate project records.

USD 2,780 to USD 6,070 a year | 4 h/week back

Finance, invoicing and collections

Manual timesheet chasing and invoice preparation

Finance staff spend three days at month-end chasing late timesheets and preparing invoices from spreadsheets.

You told us: two finance and operations staff spend about three days at month-end chasing timesheets and preparing invoices from spreadsheets, roughly 48 staff hours a month

Impact: Diverts valuable operations time to repetitive billing administration and delays collections.

Opportunity: Automate timesheet reminders and invoice drafting workflows between time-tracking and accounting tools.

USD 6,550 to USD 12,150 a year | 8.5 h/week back

Manual overdue payment reminders and delayed collections

Overdue payment reminders are sent manually, contributing to an average payment time of 52 days against 30-day terms.

You told us: Overdue payment reminders are sent manually. Clients pay in an average of 52 days against our 30-day terms, which keeps cash tight

Impact: Ties up working capital and keeps cash flow tight despite high project volume.

Opportunity: Automate payment reminder sequences and billing follow-ups.

USD 1,850 to USD 4,050 a year | 3 h/week back

People and administration

Fragmented document storage and internal searching

Documents are spread across unstructured Google Drive folders, causing staff to spend time searching for files or recreating work.

You told us: documents spread across Google Drive and no consistent structure. People spend about two hours a week searching for existing materials or recreating work

Impact: Wastes cumulative staff hours searching for internal knowledge and duplicate work.

Opportunity: Implement a structured knowledge base with searchable AI-assisted internal retrieval.

USD 19,630 to USD 36,450 a year | 25 h/week back

Data, reporting and decisions

Siloed data and lack of consolidated performance visibility

Data is spread across multiple platforms and spreadsheets, preventing founders from having a consolidated view of profitability and utilisation.

You told us: Our data is spread across Google Workspace, HubSpot, Asana, Harvest, Xero, advertising platforms and lots of spreadsheets. The founders don't have one reliable, consolidated view

Impact: Makes it difficult to track profitability across projects and contributes to lower utilisation rates.

Opportunity: Deploy an integrated data reporting dashboard that centralises metrics across tools.
