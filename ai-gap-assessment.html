AI gap assessment for Kenyan businesses

We review how your business actually works, find where AI and systemization would help, and tell you what to do about it, in what order, and at what cost.

Request my assessment

Not ready to pay yet?

Try the free AI Gap Check first. It is a ten-minute conversation that gives you a gap report and the projected return of closing the gaps. It does not prioritise the gaps or cost each opportunity. That is what this assessment adds.

Who it's for

Businesses that know AI and systemization could improve their operations but can't tell where or how to start.

What you receive

  • A written report of the opportunities we found.
  • A roadmap that puts those opportunities in priority order.
  • A cost estimate for each opportunity.
  • A walkthrough of the findings with you or your leadership.

What it costs

The fee depends on how many staff your business has. More people means more workflows to understand, more interviews and a larger roadmap.
